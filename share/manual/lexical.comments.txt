title: Comments

Part of [[lexical]].

`※` (U+203B REFERENCE MARK) begins a comment that runs to the end of the
line.  There are no block comments.

`※※` begins a **documentation comment**, which belongs to the definition
that follows it.  A run of such lines is one comment.  It is prose, and a line
beginning with `\` or `@` and a word is a Doxygen command about one part of
the definition: `\brief`, `\details`, `\param NAME`, `\return`, `\raises`,
`\pre`, `\post`, `\note`, `\warning`, `\see`, `\since`, `\deprecated` and
Doxygen's other spellings of them.  A command runs until the next begins.
Nothing else is interpreted: there is no markup.

    ※※ The larger of two numbers.
    ※※
    ※※ \param a one number
    ※※ \param b the other
    ※※ \return whichever is larger
    fn larger(a: u8, b: u8) → u8:
        a ⌈ b                     ※ an ordinary comment

The compiler checks what a command claims and warns where it is wrong: a
`\param` naming no parameter (4601) or one named twice (4602), a `\return`
on a function that answers nothing (4603), either on a definition that is
not a function (4604), and a word that is no command (4600).  It does not ask
for a `\param` per parameter.

The library's documentation is its `※※` comments: this manual's nodes of
[[library]] are made from them.  A plain `※` comment among the `※※` lines is
a remark about how the thing is made, and stays out of the manual.
