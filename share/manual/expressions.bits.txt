title: Bits

Part of [[expressions]].

    a & b   a | b   a ^ b   ~a     and, or, exclusive or, complement
    a « n   a » n                  shift left and right
    a ↺ n   a ↻ n                  rotate left and right
    ⎕ones(a)  ⎕lead(a)  ⎕trail(a)  bits set, zeroes above, zeroes below
    ⎕wide_mul(a, b)                the whole 128-bit product of two 64-bit numbers
    ⎕clmul(a, b)                   the same without carries, of two u64

**The bitwise operators take whole numbers** -- and the values of a flag
enumeration ([[types.enums]]) -- with both sides of one type: `1u8 & 2u16` is
refused.  A truth value has no bits; `∧ ∨ ¬` are what apply to it.  They bind
tighter than a comparison, so `a & mask = mask` asks about `a & mask`.

**A shift** drops what falls off the end; a signed number shifted right brings
in copies of its sign.  The distance is of the same type, and **a distance of
the width or more stops the program** (status 80) -- or is taken modulo the
width inside `⎕wrap` ([[expressions.arithmetic]]).  **A rotation** turns the
bits of the type, not of the register, and is defined on unsigned types only:
`13u8 ↻ 1u8` is `134u8`.  Shifts and rotations bind where `×` does.

**Counting** answers a `u8` over the type's own width: `⎕ones(⁻1i8)` is 8,
`⎕lead(1u8)` is 7, and `⎕lead` and `⎕trail` of nought are the width.
`⎕trail` of a mask is the place of its lowest set bit, which is how a walk
over the set bits goes: take it, then clear it with `m & (m - 1)`.

**`⎕wide_mul(a, b)`** multiplies two `u64` or two `i64` (4243) and answers
`〈upper, lower〉`, the lower half always a `u64`: `let high, low :=
⎕wide_mul(x, y)`.  It never fails.  **`⎕clmul(a, b)`** is the product
without carries -- of polynomials over bits, a CRC's and GHASH's -- of two
`u64` (4020), answered the same way; `⎕clmul(x, ~0u64)`'s lower half is the
prefix exclusive or of `x`.  One instruction at x86-64-v3 and with RISC-V's
Zbc or Zbkc, ordinary multiplications elsewhere.

**`⎕mask(b)`** makes the bits of a number out of an array of truth values
([[types.arrays.masks]]).

Program:

    let std := ⎕import("std")

    fn places(m: u16) → [u8]:
        let out: mut [u8] = []
        let rest: mut u16 = m
        while rest ≠ 0u16:
            out ⧺← [⎕trail(rest)]
            rest ← rest & (rest - 1u16)
        out

    @[startup]
    fn main(init: std.Init) → u6:
        let m: u16 = 0b1010_0000_0110u16
        let high, low := ⎕wide_mul(1u64 « 63u64, 4u64)
        let square := ⎕clmul(0b1011u64, 0b1011u64)⟦1⟧
        _ ← std.println⌜&mut init.io.output, "{} {} {} {} {} {}", places(m), ⎕ones(m), 13u8 ↻ 1u8, high, low, square⌝
        0u6

Output:

    [1, 2, 9, 11] 4 134 2 0 69
