title: std.io

Part of [[library]], handed on by [[std]].  The devices a program reads and
writes: **`Reader`**, **`Writer`** and **`ReadWriter`**, each a descriptor
and where the ring its requests go through is.  A device is `@[device]`, so
holding one is permission to do I/O through it, and `@[closed_by(close)]`, so
one that was opened is moved, never copied, and closed by whoever holds it
last -- `defer out.close()` where it is bound ([[memory.unique]]).  The three
a process starts with are fields of `std.Init`, lent and never closed.

**`Pending`** is a request that has been started and may not have been
answered: `std.write` answers one and `std.flush` waits for it.  **`Answer`**
is what a task is handed back when the kernel has answered its request,
`std.done` and `std.received` reading it ([[functions.coroutines]]).

The rest -- `Ring`, the `start` functions, `Pending.wait`, `taken` and `ready`
-- is what `std` and `std.fs` are built on; a program uses `std`'s functions.
Every request goes through `io_uring` where the kernel has it, and straight to
the kernel where it has not, which a program cannot tell apart.
