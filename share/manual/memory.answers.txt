title: Where an answer was made

Part of [[memory]].

**`→ T in a` says what a function answers was made in the arena parameter
`a`**, or in something lasting longer -- `⎕heap`, the image.  A caller takes
the answer to live as long as what it handed for `a`; the body is held to it,
an answer made anywhere else being copied there before the function leaves
(4624 where it cannot be copied).  **Saying nothing means `⎕heap`.**

    fn label(a: &mut arena, b: &mut arena, v: u16) → str in a:
        let scratch: str = ⍕v in b          ※ working space
        "#" ⧺ scratch in a                  ※ the answer

    fn head(xs: u8⟦⟧) → u8⟦⟧ in xs:         ※ some of what xs holds
        xs⟦0…2⟧

- `→ T in a, b` -- the answer is from either, and lives as long as the shorter.
- `→ T in ⎕heap` -- always the heap: the answer travels without its allocator.
- `→ T in ⎕static` -- always the image; nothing else may be answered (4635).
- `→ T in a, ⎕static` -- the arena or the image, text written down being
  answered as it is.  `std.text` says this.
- **`s: T in a`** on a parameter -- what is handed was made in what `a` is
  handed (4620); **`s: T in ⎕static`** -- in the image (4649).

What follows `in` is a parameter holding an arena, `&mut arena` or `&arena`
(4618), or `⎕heap` or `⎕static`; on a type that points nowhere it says nothing
(4621).  It is a lifetime spelled as the arena -- what `⧖a` says of a
reference ([[memory.lifetimes]]).

**A function may hand an arena of its own out with its answer**: `→ T in
pool`, where `pool` is no parameter, says the body makes `let pool: mut arena
= ⎕arena` (4627) and answers something made in it.  Only a definition receives
it, naming the arena (4629):

    fn words(text: str) → [str] in pool:
        let pool: mut arena = ⎕arena
        …

    let found: [str] in kept = words(line)      ※ kept is now the caller's

Nothing is copied: the arena starts where the caller will hold it.  `f(…) in
kept` is refused (4630), as is a definition with `in` whose value is no such
call (4631).

**Writing into a place from outside the call** -- a field a reference
parameter names, an element of an array handed in -- is held to what an
answer is: made in `⎕heap` or the image, or copied into `⎕heap`.

Program:

    let std := ⎕import("std")

    fn shout(text: str) → str in pool:
        let pool: mut arena = ⎕arena
        let out: mut str = "" ⧺ text in pool
        foreach _ := 0u8…3u8:
            out ← out ⧺ "!" in pool
        out

    @[startup]
    fn main(init: std.Init) → u6:
        let loud: str in kept = shout("hey")
        _ ← std.println⌜&mut init.io.output, "{}", loud⌝
        0u6

Output:

    hey!!!
